Business reporting

Teams disagree on the numbers? Start with definitions.

Before adding a chart, check each KPI's calculation, scope and source. The same label can describe two different realities. Documentation and business validation matter as much as the dashboard.

Next stepPick one critical KPI and have the teams using it agree on its definition.

02
Cloud & Data

Azure, Databricks or Fabric: start with needs, not a logo.

Your sources, skills, processing needs and BI environment should guide the architecture. These platforms can address different or complementary needs: compare governance, operations and costs rather than looking for one universal winner.

Next stepList your sources, users, required data freshness and access constraints before discussing a platform.

03
AI adoption

A useful first AI pilot has clear boundaries.

Choose a repetitive task with permitted inputs and someone who can verify the output. Compare AI assistance with a simpler process change. Define exceptions and stop criteria before expanding use.

Next stepUse the AI Pilot Canvas to scope one task with its business owner.

04
Team enablement

Training should look like your team's actual work.

HR, finance and managers do not use AI in the same way. Role-specific exercises, non-sensitive data and verification criteria help people see where tools can help—and where human judgment remains essential.

Next stepIdentify one task each team wants to handle better and build the exercise around it.
