Before we work together

Your questions, answered.

Where should we start if the need is not yet clear?

A first 20-minute conversation explores your objective, sources and constraints. We can then propose a diagnostic or a scoped project.

Can you work with our existing environment?

We start with your tools and skills. Our offer covers Power BI, Tableau, Azure, Databricks and Microsoft Fabric; the scope depends on your sources, access and selected architecture.

How will our data be protected?

A confidentiality agreement precedes data access. No data is sent to an AI tool without written agreement. Access rights and permitted processing are defined with you.

Can you support our team in French?

Yes. Workshops and support are available in English and French. Materials, handover and documentation needs are agreed during scoping.

Trust and accountability

Your goals guide the project. Scope, permitted data and review points are defined together.
